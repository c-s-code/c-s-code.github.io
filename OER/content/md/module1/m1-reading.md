## Reading for this Module

Sian Bayne (2024) ‘Digital education utopia’, _Learning, Media and Technology_, 49(3), pp. 506–521. Available at: <a href="https://doi.org/10.1080/17439884.2023.2262382" target="_blank">https://doi.org/10.1080/17439884.2023.2262382</a>.

**Please read pages 506 to 510.**
Bayne uses Ruth Levitas’s 2013 ‘Utopia as Method’ book to demonstrate how utopian thinking operates as a methodological lens to interrogate the history, ontology, and future of a platformised digital education landscape.
