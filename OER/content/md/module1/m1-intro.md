Utopia as method is exactly that – a methodology that attempts to make the implicit explicit and ‘disrupts the closure of the present’ (Levitas, 2013, p. 119). As such, it makes for an appropriate vehicle to examine the hidden curricula that shape digital education.

In this module you will learn about Levitas’s three modes of archaeology, ontology, and architecture through Bayne’s 2024 Open Access ‘Digital education utopia’ paper which tackles Utopia as method for digital education.

## Learning Outcomes

> By the end of this module, you will be able to:
>
> - Explain the ‘good society’ in the context of futures methods
> - Apply Levitas's three modes of utopia as method to analyse a digital educational context
