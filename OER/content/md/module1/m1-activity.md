## Activity

Consider a digital education tool (or tools) in your context (eg Canvas, Learn, MS365, Zoom).

### Part 1 – Archaeology and Ontology

Analyse the utopia of the present by considering current discourse, imaginaries and policies.

1. **Archaeology** – What promises does/did this tool make?
   - What shape does that utopia take – what does it promise? (digital transformation, future-proof etc)
   - How does that contrast with your experiences?
2. **Ontology** – Consider the subjects of this utopia. Remember – students are not the only subjects.
   - How does this tool frame subject relationships? (consumer-vendor, student-teacher etc)
   - What kinds of futures are opened or closed through this ontology?
3. **Opportunities**
   - Where does the status quo obstruct for novelty or growth? What practices, policies, or designs close these off?
   - Propose one small intervention that might enable new opportunities?
