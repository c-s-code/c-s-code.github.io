## Welcome

This Open Educational Resource is designed to take you approximately 3 hours to complete, including time for reading, reflection, and completing activites and sharing your work on Padlet boards.

You will read through a very short introduction to contextualise the course, then proceed to learn about 'Utopia as method', employ that to uncover hidden curricula of resilience, finally architect open source futures for digital education.

Each page indicates an expected time commitment for each piece of work, but please go through as quickly or as slowly as you would like.
