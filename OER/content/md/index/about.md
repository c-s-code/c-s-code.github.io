## About me

I am Chris, an Accessibility Specialist at HMRC.

I have nine years experience in education - delivering high-quality, inclusive, accessible artefacts and multi-modal projects from ideation through to implementation.

I taught English as a second language (ESL) in primary and early secondary (ages 7-15) for five years, before joining the Digital Education Unit at the Royal (Dick) School of Veterinary Studies.

Throughout my carreer I have interacted with many digital platforms and experienced the impact their (mis)use can have.
