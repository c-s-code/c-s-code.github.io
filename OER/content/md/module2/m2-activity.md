## Activity - The Good Resilience Society

Consider digital education’s subjects and objects: students, educators, institutions, technology, policy makers, and more.

### Analyse

Choose one framing of resilience from this module (or propose your own) and analyse it in your context:

1. **Archaeology** – How is it framed in your context? What does its hidden curriculum reveal about a ‘good society’ for digital education.
2. **Ontology** – What opportunities or possibilities emerge/vanish through this framing? Which aspects are neglected? Whose futures are absent from mainstream narratives? What kind of subjects does your uncovered ‘good society’ produce?
