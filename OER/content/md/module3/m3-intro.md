In this module, you will architect an intervention towards an open source ‘good society’ future. This will be a quick look into novel, optimistic, and open futures for digital education (Levitas, 2013, p. 226). It is not a concrete plan, nor even a proposal, but it should help you think of new ways to approach blockers on the road to your vision of resilience for digital education.

## Learning Outcomes

> **By the end of this module, you will be able to:**
>
> - Architect an open-source ‘good society’ that shifts digital education resilience to a positive trajectory
> - Evaluate an architected intervention, applying archaeological and ontological evaluation
