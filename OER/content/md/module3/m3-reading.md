## Reading for this Module

European Comission (2020) ‘Open source software strategy 2020-2023’. Brussels. Available at: <a href="https://commission.europa.eu/about/departments-and-executive-agencies/digital-services/open-source-software-strategy_en" target="_blank">https://commission.europa.eu/about/departments-and-executive-agencies/digital-services/open-source-software-strategy_en</a>.

**Read sections 4 and 5.**
Under the theme ‘Think Open’, the commission sets out a digital strategy that rejects opaque proprietary algorithms and ‘hyperscalers’ in favour of open source alternatives that allow knowledge-sharing, collaboration, and ‘digital sovereignty’.
