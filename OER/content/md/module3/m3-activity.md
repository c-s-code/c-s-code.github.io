## Activity - A Good (Open Source) Society

Look back at your module 2 activity output and architect an open source ‘good society’ to affect positive change. Consider what barriers exist that impede your desired future, and what guiding principles might help you break those down.

### Identify a Barrier

List an immediate barrier preventing the architectural change you desire. Make a note of:

- Type: Technical, institutional, cultural, policy…
- Controller: Platform vendor, institution, government…
- What would it take to overcome?

### Intervention

Propose a small intervention you could implement immediately, assuming near-unlimited local influence. You can choose to align with one of the European Commission’s six principles. Some examples:

- (Think Open) Transition away from Microsoft 365 or Google productivity suites to open source alternatives.
- (Transform/Contribute) Develop and launch a new open source Learning Technology Integration for your LMS that adds frequently requested functionality.

### Stakeholders and Resources

- Collaborators: Colleagues, student groups, unions, professional bodies…
- Resources: Funding, policy support, infrastructure…

### Reflect

- Apply archaeological and ontological modes to evaluate your intervention.
- Ask: How does your ‘good society’ shift the resilience of digital education?
