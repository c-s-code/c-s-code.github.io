Welcome to this Open Educational Resource. In this course you will explore the concept of resilience, tackling it as a hidden curriculum shaping education, and how you might architect your own ‘good society’ to take back control of digital education.

## Learning Outcomes

> On completion of this OER, you will be able to:
>
> - Explain the ‘good society’ in the context of futures methods
> - Apply Levitas's three modes of utopia as method to analyse a digital educational context
> - Identify and critically interrogate hidden curricula operating within digital education systems
> - Architect an open-source ‘good society’ that shifts digital education resilience to a positive trajectory
> - Evaluate an architected intervention, applying archaeological and ontological evaluation
